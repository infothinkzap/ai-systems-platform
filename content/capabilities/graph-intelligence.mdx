Graph Intelligence is our fundamental approach to reasoning over disjointed enterprise data. Instead of forcing data into rigid relational tables, we preserve its natural connections.

When an invoice is extracted, it isn't just saved—it is mapped as a node, connected to vendor nodes, transaction nodes, and contract nodes. This topology allows our downstream systems (like the AI CFO) to walk the graph and understand context, rather than just extracting text.

*This capability strictly operates on data primitives defined in the semantic ontology. Those relationships are loaded from the graph, not hardcoded here.*
