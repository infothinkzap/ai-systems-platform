The AI CFO represents the synthesis of deterministic financial logic and probabilistic reasoning. It is designed for modern finance leaders who need to transition from historical reporting to predictive, real-time financial intelligence.

By connecting directly to raw data streams (invoices, transactions) and structuring them via our Graph Intelligence capability, the system uncovers hidden cashflow patterns and anomalies before they compound into systemic risks.

### The Architecture of Financial Reasoning

At its core, the AI CFO does not just query databases. It maps financial realities into a structured ontology, allowing the reasoning engine to trace a single vendor payment back to an originating contract, automatically flagging deviations.
