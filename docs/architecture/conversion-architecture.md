# Conversion Architecture

The conversion funnel is designed to move a visitor from passive understanding to active engagement, culminating in a commercial conversation.

## The Conceptual Funnel

1. **VISITOR**: Arrives via thought-leadership, search, or direct link.
2. **UNDERSTAND**: Reads the "Problem-First" philosophy. Realizes Terrawave builds robust systems, not fragile wrappers.
3. **EXPLORE**: Navigates Systems or Capabilities based on structural interest.
4. **RECOGNIZE PROBLEM**: The critical moment. The visitor sees their exact friction point articulated (e.g., "Fragmented WhatsApp Leads").
5. **TRUST**: Views the System Architecture, the strict Evidence status ("Prototype tested"), and Founder builder profiles.
6. **START CONVERSATION**: Clicks the primary CTA.
7. **DISCOVERY**: A structured, asynchronous qualification step (form or interactive flow).
8. **AI OPPORTUNITY MAPPING**: Terrawave analyzes the input.
9. **SYSTEM PROPOSAL**: Sales/Engineering conversation.
10. **IMPLEMENTATION**: Commercial engagement.

## Calls to Action (CTAs)

### Primary CTA
- **Text**: "Start a Conversation" or "Discover Your AI Opportunity"
- **Placement**: Persistent in header, end of Homepage, end of System/Service pages.
- **Action**: Opens the Problem Discovery Form.

### Secondary CTAs
- **Text**: "Explore Architecture", "Read Case Study", "See How We Think"
- **Placement**: Contextual within pages to drive deeper graph traversal.

## The Problem Discovery Form (Pre-Sales)
To keep the initial website free and frictionless, the conversion mechanism should be a highly structured form (e.g., Tally, Typeform, or custom static form hooked to a free tier email/webhook service).

**Information Collected (The "Discovery"):**
- Name, Role, Company.
- "What is the primary operational friction you are facing?" (Open text or categorized by our Ontology problems).
- "What data or software currently lives around this problem?" (Mapping to our Data Sources).
- "What is the desired outcome?" (Mapping to Outcomes).

This allows Terrawave to enter the first sales conversation having already mapped the client to the semantic ontology.
