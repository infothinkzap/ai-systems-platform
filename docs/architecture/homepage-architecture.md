# Homepage Architecture

The homepage is a conceptual funnel designed to quickly establish positioning, demonstrate thinking, show proof, and invite problem discovery.

## The Sequence

### 1. The Hero (Positioning)
- **Purpose**: Immediately state what Terrawave is and who it's for.
- **Answers**: "What is Terrawave?"
- **Graph Entity**: `org:terrawave`
- **Desired Action**: Scroll to understand the philosophy.

### 2. The Problem-First Idea (Thinking)
- **Purpose**: Contrast Terrawave against generic AI hype. We don't start with LLMs; we start with business friction.
- **Answers**: "How do they think about AI?"
- **Graph Entity**: `phil:problem_first`, `phil:systems_thinking`
- **Desired Action**: Agree with the premise and want to see the model.

### 3. The AI System Model (Architecture)
- **Purpose**: Visually explain the Data Flow (Source → Primitive → Capability → Decision).
- **Answers**: "How do they actually build intelligence?"
- **Graph Entity**: The flow from `DataSource` to `Capability` to `Outcome`.
- **Desired Action**: Understand that we build rigorous systems, not just chatbots.

### 4. Problem Discovery Routing (The Hook)
- **Purpose**: Let the visitor self-identify. E.g., tabs or cards: "Too much manual reconciliation", "Fragmented lead data", "Complex custom workflows".
- **Answers**: "Do they understand a problem I have?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Persona` → `Problem`
- **Desired Action**: Click through to a System or Service.

### 5. Systems We Build (Product Mode)
- **Purpose**: Showcase packaged intelligence.
- **Answers**: "What have they built?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Product` (AI CFO, CA Automation, Lead Intelligence). 
- *Note*: Ensure `status` and `evidence_level` are clearly displayed.
- **Desired Action**: Explore a system page.

### 6. Capabilities & Engineering (Technical Depth)
- **Purpose**: Show the underlying primitives we master.
- **Answers**: "What capabilities do they possess?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Capability` (Document Intel, Graph Intel).
- **Desired Action**: Trust our technical competence.

### 7. Custom Services (Systems Mode)
- **Purpose**: Catch visitors whose problems don't fit a pre-built system.
- **Answers**: "How can my business engage them for custom work?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Service` (Opportunity Discovery, System Design).
- **Desired Action**: Initiate a Discovery Session.

### 8. Evidence & Case Studies
- **Purpose**: Prove the claims using the Epistemic Status framework.
- **Answers**: "Can they actually do this?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Evidence` mapped to `Product` or `Service`.
- **Desired Action**: Trust Terrawave.

### 9. The Founders
- **Purpose**: Humanize the company and establish builder credibility.
- **Answers**: "Who is behind this?"
- **Graph Entity**: `Person:adarsh`, `Person:rahul`
- **Desired Action**: Feel comfortable reaching out.

### 10. The Conversion (Next Step)
- **Purpose**: Drive the primary CTA.
- **Answers**: "What should I do next?"
- **Desired Action**: Fill out the Discovery / Work With Us form.
