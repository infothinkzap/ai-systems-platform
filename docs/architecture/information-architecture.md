# Information Architecture

The Terrawave Information Architecture (IA) translates the Master Knowledge Graph into a human-centered traversal experience. The website is not a product catalogue; it is a positioning, credibility, and problem-discovery system.

## Dual Structure Framework

The website operates on two simultaneous structures:
1. **Structural Navigation**: How users browse (Home, Think, Systems, Capabilities, Services, Case Studies, About, Work With Us).
2. **Semantic Navigation**: How users understand Terrawave (Person → Problem → Workflow → Friction → Capability → System/Service → Outcome → Evidence → Conversation).

The semantic navigation is prioritized. The goal is to move the visitor from self-identifying a problem to initiating a conversation.

## Top-Level Sections

### 1. Home
The entry point. Answers "Who are these people, how do they think, what do they build, and how can they help me?" (See `homepage-architecture.md`)

### 2. Think
The philosophical and structural core of Terrawave. 
- *Purpose*: Establish credibility and thought leadership.
- *Content*: Evergreen manifestos and deep-dives into Problem-First AI, Systems Thinking, Human + AI Systems, Determinism vs GenAI.
- *Connection*: Every "Think" piece must link to a real System or Case Study demonstrating the principle in action.

### 3. Systems
The Product Mode showcase. 
- *Purpose*: Demonstrate our ability to package capabilities into reusable systems.
- *Content*: AI CFO, CA Automation, Lead Intelligence, ExamOS, SelfOS.
- *Connection*: Maps Problems to Outcomes using Capabilities.

### 4. Capabilities
The technical primitives we wield. 
- *Purpose*: Prove technical depth without becoming a meaningless API catalogue.
- *Content*: Document Intelligence, Conversation Understanding, Graph Intelligence, etc.
- *Connection*: A capability page must never exist in a vacuum; it must show the *Data Primitive* it consumes, and the *System* that uses it.

### 5. Services
The Systems Mode showcase. 
- *Purpose*: Offer custom problem-solving for unique client friction.
- *Content*: AI Opportunity Discovery, System Design, Integration.
- *Connection*: Maps Client Journey from Friction to Implementation.

### 6. Problem Discovery (The Commercial Core)
A dynamic or guided routing experience. 
- *Purpose*: Allow a visitor to self-identify their friction (e.g., "I have too much repetitive accounting work") and route them to the appropriate System or Service.
- *Connection*: The bridge between the visitor's context and our solutions.

### 7. Case Studies / Evidence
The proof layer. 
- *Purpose*: Validate claims using the strict Evidence Framework (Prototype, Experiment, Production).
- *Content*: Deconstructions of built systems, identifying the problem, human role, software role, and AI role.

### 8. About & Work With Us
The human and conversion layer. 
- *Purpose*: Introduce the founders as Problem Solvers and AI Builders, and provide a low-friction path to conversation.
