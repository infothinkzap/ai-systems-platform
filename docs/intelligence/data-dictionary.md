# Data Dictionary

This dictionary outlines the explicit Data Primitives that flow through Terrawave systems, and their specific `DataSources` (Provenance). AI systems do not operate in a vacuum; they consume, transform, and generate specific structured and unstructured data originating from concrete sources.

## Business Intelligence Data

### AI CFO
- **Transactions**: Raw financial movements. *Originates from: ERP, Bank APIs, CSV imports.*
- **Financial Statements**: Aggregated views of financial health. *Originates from: Accounting Software.*
- **Vendor Records**: Historical data concerning suppliers. *Originates from: ERP, CRM.*
- **Invoices**: Unstructured/semi-structured bills. *Originates from: Email attachments, PDF uploads.*
- **Payments**: Records of settled invoices. *Originates from: Bank feeds, ERP.*
- **Accounting Entries**: Structured journal entries. *Originates from: ERP.*

### CA Automation
- **Invoices**: Raw input documents requiring OCR. *Originates from: Email, Image uploads, PDF scans.*
- **GST Data**: Tax specific records. *Originates from: Government Tax Portals, APIs.*
- **TDS Data**: Tax Deducted at Source records. *Originates from: TRACES portal.*
- **Vendor Data**: Master data for reconciliation. *Originates from: ERP, CSV.*
- **Reconciliation Records**: Outputs of matching processes. *Originates from: Internal Intelligence Processing.*
- **Supporting Documents**: Contracts, receipts, e-way bills. *Originates from: Email, Drive folders.*

### Lead Intelligence
- **WhatsApp Conversations**: Unstructured chat data. *Originates from: WhatsApp Business API.*
- **Instagram Conversations**: Direct messages. *Originates from: Instagram Graph API.*
- **Lead Profiles**: Structured records. *Originates from: CRM, derived from intent signals.*
- **Intent Signals**: Extracted buying intent tags. *Originates from: Derived by Conversation Intelligence.*

## Knowledge Intelligence Data

### ExamOS
- **Syllabus**: Structural hierarchy of topics. *Originates from: PDF uploads, Web scraping.*
- **Questions**: Assessment items. *Originates from: Question bank databases, generated by AI.*
- **Performance Data**: Quantitative metrics of student capability. *Originates from: Application interaction logs.*
- **Knowledge Graph Nodes**: Semantic relationships. *Originates from: Derived by Graph Intelligence.*

## Personal Intelligence Data

### SelfOS
- **Goals & Preferences**: Implicit/explicit user constraints. *Originates from: User Input, UI toggles.*
- **Routines**: Temporal behavior patterns. *Originates from: Device OS APIs, Calendar integrations.*
- **Context**: Real-time state. *Originates from: Geolocation APIs, time, connected device state.*
