# Domain Dictionary

Terrawave operates across three primary conceptual **System Domains**. These domains categorize our intelligence systems and products based on the context and scale of the problems they solve.

Within these domains, we operate in two modes: **Product Mode** (reusable systems) and **Systems Mode** (custom analysis, design, and integration services). See `offering-dictionary.md` for details.

## 1. Business Intelligence
**Focus**: Complex organizational workflows, financial data, compliance, and revenue generation.

Business systems deal with high-stakes, structured and unstructured corporate data. They require extreme reliability, deterministic validation, and human-in-the-loop exception handling.
- **Key Personas**: Business Owner, CFO / Finance Leader, Accounting Team, Sales / Marketing Team.
- **Key Outcomes**: Reduced manual workload, reliable workflow automation, better financial decisions, conversation to action.

### Associated Products:
- **AI CFO**: Solves financial complexity by creating institutional financial memory and relationship graphs.
- **CA Automation**: Solves accounting and compliance bottlenecks (GST/TDS reconciliation, invoice processing).
- **Lead Intelligence**: Solves fragmented lead conversations across WhatsApp/Instagram.

## 2. Knowledge Intelligence
**Focus**: Specialized knowledge acquisition, synthesis, structuring, and education.

Knowledge systems transform static, overwhelming information (like syllabi or corporate documentation) into dynamic, personalized learning and synthesis engines.
- **Key Personas**: Competitive Exam Aspirant, Student, Researcher.
- **Key Outcomes**: Personalized exam preparation, knowledge retention, accelerated syllabus coverage.

### Associated Products:
- **ExamOS**: Adaptive, personalized exam preparation and knowledge graph system.

## 3. Personal Intelligence
**Focus**: Hyper-personalized contextual assistance for individuals.

Personal systems focus on understanding an individual deeply—their goals, routines, preferences, and personal knowledge—to provide proactive, personalized action and decision assistance.
- **Key Personas**: Individual, Knowledge Worker.
- **Key Outcomes**: Context-aware AI assistance, routine adherence, task completion.

### Associated Products:
- **SelfOS**: Personalized operating system built around individual context and routines.
