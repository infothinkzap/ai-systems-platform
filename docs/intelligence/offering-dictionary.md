# Offering Dictionary

Terrawave operates in two complementary modes: **Product Mode** and **Systems Mode**. We do not merely sell predefined AI products; we analyze, design, and integrate intelligence into complex environments.

## 1. Product Mode
Building and deploying reusable, packaged AI systems for recurring, well-defined industry problems.

### Products
- **AI CFO**: Financial intelligence and decision support system.
- **CA Automation**: Accounting, compliance, GST/TDS reconciliation, and document intelligence.
- **Lead Intelligence**: Omnichannel lead capture, structuring, and qualification engine.
- **ExamOS**: Adaptive, personalized exam preparation and knowledge graph system.
- **SelfOS**: Personalized operating system around individual context and routines.

*Relationships*: `PRODUCT_SOLVES`

## 2. Systems Mode (Services)
Analyzing a client's unique problem, workflow, and friction points to design, build, and integrate custom AI architecture. These are our commercial service offerings.

### Services
- **AI Opportunity Discovery**: Identifying and scoping high-leverage areas for AI intervention within an existing business.
- **AI Workflow Analysis**: Deconstructing current state workflows to map human judgment, repetition, and data flow.
- **AI System Design**: Architecting the intelligence, data, and action layers required to solve a specific problem.
- **Custom AI System Development**: Building bespoke intelligent systems tailored to unique organizational needs.
- **AI Integration**: Connecting AI capabilities into existing enterprise software and databases.
- **AI Automation**: Implementing deterministic execution layers driven by AI reasoning.
- **AI Transformation / Implementation**: End-to-end organizational change management and deployment of intelligence systems.

*Relationships*: `SERVICE_ADDRESSES`, `SERVICE_SUPPORTS`, `SERVICE_CUSTOMIZES`, `SERVICE_INTEGRATES`
