# Outcomes and Metrics

This document separates qualitative outcomes (the human or business benefit) from the quantitative metrics (the measurable data points) that prove those outcomes. Terrawave defines the metric types, but does not invent actual performance values for systems in development.

## Business Intelligence

### Outcome: Reduced Manual Workload
The reduction of repetitive, low-leverage human effort in administrative tasks.
*Measured By:*
- **Hours Saved**: Total human hours recovered per workflow cycle.
- **Processing Time**: End-to-end time taken to complete a specific task.
- **Automation Rate**: Percentage of a workflow completed without human intervention.

### Outcome: Reliable Workflow Automation
The ability to trust an automated system to handle exceptions and edge cases deterministically.
*Measured By:*
- **Error Rate**: Percentage of processed items requiring correction.
- **Exception Resolution Rate**: Percentage of edge cases successfully handled by the human-in-the-loop escalation path.
- **Reconciliation Match Rate**: Percentage of records matched automatically.

### Outcome: Better Financial Decisions
Strategic clarity derived from contextual, real-time financial memory and graphs.
*Measured By:*
- **Time to Insight**: How fast a specific strategic question can be answered.
- **Data Coverage**: The percentage of historical financial data successfully mapped into the graph.

### Outcome: Conversation to Action (High Conversion)
Transforming unstructured chats into structured business value.
*Measured By:*
- **Lead Qualification Speed**: Time from first message to qualification status.
- **Routing Accuracy**: Percentage of leads routed to the correct agent or workflow.
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of captured leads that convert to a desired action.

## Knowledge & Personal Intelligence

### Outcome: Personalized Exam Preparation
Moving away from one-size-fits-all study plans to adaptive learning.
*Measured By:*
- **Knowledge Retention Rate**: Long-term memory strength on specific syllabus nodes.
- **Assessment Score Improvement**: Delta in practice test performance over time.
- **Syllabus Coverage Speed**: Rate at which a student successfully completes syllabus nodes.

### Outcome: Context-Aware AI Assistance
An operating system that understands the individual.
*Measured By:*
- **Context Retrieval Accuracy**: How often the AI utilizes correct historical context in an interaction.
- **Routine Adherence**: Measurable consistency in user-defined routines.
- **Task Completion Rate**: Percentage of tracked goals and tasks achieved.
