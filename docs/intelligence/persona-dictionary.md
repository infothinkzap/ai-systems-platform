# Persona Dictionary

This dictionary defines the specific User Personas that interact with or benefit from Terrawave systems. A system must always be contextualized by the person using it, the problem they have, and the outcome they desire.

## Business Intelligence Personas

### 1. CFO / Finance Leader
- **Description**: The strategic head of finance dealing with organizational complexity, cash flow, and high-level decision making.
- **Primary Problem**: Financial Complexity, scattered institutional memory, and delayed insights.
- **Uses System**: AI CFO
- **Desired Outcome**: Better, faster financial decisions and comprehensive institutional financial memory.

### 2. Chartered Accountant / Accounting Team
- **Description**: The operational finance professional handling reconciliations, compliance, and vendor management.
- **Primary Problem**: Repetitive accounting and compliance work, exception handling, and data extraction from unstructured invoices.
- **Uses System**: CA Automation
- **Desired Outcome**: Reliable workflow automation, reduced manual workload, and zero-error compliance.

### 3. Business Owner / Executive
- **Description**: The leader of a business seeking to resolve friction, scale operations, or uncover new efficiencies.
- **Primary Problem**: Undefined operational friction and disconnected workflows.
- **Engages With**: AI Opportunity Discovery, AI System Design (Systems Mode)
- **Desired Outcome**: Measurable ROI, scalable operations, and strategic AI transformation.

### 4. Sales / Marketing Team & Lead Operator
- **Description**: The individuals responsible for capturing, qualifying, and converting inbound interest into revenue.
- **Primary Problem**: Fragmented lead conversations across WhatsApp/Instagram, delayed follow-ups, and unstructured intent data.
- **Uses System**: Lead Intelligence
- **Desired Outcome**: High conversion rates, structured conversation data, and automated routing.

## Knowledge Intelligence Personas

### 5. Competitive Exam Aspirant / Student
- **Description**: An individual attempting to master a vast, complex syllabus under time constraints.
- **Primary Problem**: Fragmented exam preparation, one-size-fits-all study plans, and inefficient revision.
- **Uses System**: ExamOS
- **Desired Outcome**: Personalized exam preparation, adaptive learning paths, and improved assessment scores.

## Personal Intelligence Personas

### 6. Individual / Knowledge Worker
- **Description**: A person seeking to optimize their daily life, knowledge consumption, and routine decisions.
- **Primary Problem**: Generic tools that don't understand the individual's unique context, goals, or preferences.
- **Uses System**: SelfOS
- **Desired Outcome**: An operating system built around the individual, continuous learning, and context-aware AI assistance.
